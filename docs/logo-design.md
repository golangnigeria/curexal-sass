# Curexal Brand Identity & Logo Guidelines

This document serves as the official design guide and specification sheet for the Curexal (Curexal space) brand logo assets. Every application, dashboard, print material, and digital interface must adhere to these guidelines to maintain a premium and cohesive corporate identity.

---

## 1. Brand Concept & Philosophy

The Curexal brand represents a modern, cloud-native **Healthcare Operating System** that unifies diagnostic ecosystems. The visual identity balances scientific precision with enterprise technology, presenting as a **technology company first** and a **healthcare provider second**.

### The Symbol
The Curexal brand mark is an abstract geometric symbol constructed of three interlocking, continuous loops suggesting a digital network topology. 
*   **Trust & Connectivity:** The loops interlock perfectly, representing secure data handoff between clinics, laboratories, and patients.
*   **Precision:** Built on a strict geometric grid to reflect medical accuracy and reliability.
*   **Scalability:** The minimal, clean vector construct is easily readable from a 24px favicon to large scale signage.

## 3. Typography & Wordmark

The Curexal wordmark utilizes a clean, modern geometric sans-serif typeface to communicate technical sophistication and accessibility.

*   **Primary Typeface:** `Inter` or `Space Grotesk` / `Söhne` (medium weight).
*   **Text Layout:** The wordmark should read: `Curexal` or `Curexal space`.
*   **Spacing:** Tracking/letter-spacing must be set to slightly tight (`tracking-tight` or `-0.02em`) to maintain a unified compact look.
*   **Casing:** Title Case (`Curexal`). Never display the wordmark in all caps or all lowercase.

## 5. Technical Design Standards

To ensure the brand assets look crisp across print, web, and mobile contexts, they must be constructed under strict constraints:

*   **Vector Construction:** The symbol is built purely on a flat vector structure.
*   **No Gradients or 3D Effects:** Do not apply lighting layers, bevels, shadows, or metallic gradients. 
*   **Scalability Standards:**
    *   **Favicon / Mobile App Icon:** Use the **Symbol Only** version. Minimum size is 16px.
    *   **Desktop Dashboards / Sidebars:** Use the **Primary Horizontal Logo** configuration.
    *   **Print / Reports / Invoices:** Use the **Stacked Logo** layout or **Monochrome Logo** depending on ink/contrast constraints.
*   **Embroidery & Print Friendly:** Designed with clean boundary line definitions, making it suitable for lab coats, corporate banners, and physical letterheads.
